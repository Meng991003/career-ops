Career-Ops

An AI-driven job-search pipeline that runs inside your AI coding CLI (Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, Gemini/Antigravity…). It evaluates job offers, writes tailored CVs & cover letters, scans company career portals, and tracks every application — so you apply to fewer, better-matched roles.

01What this repo actually is

It's not a web app you launch. It's a folder of instructions + Node scripts that an AI agent reads and runs on your behalf. You talk to it in plain English in your CLI; it reads your CV and profile, then executes the right "mode." The philosophy: companies use AI to filter candidates — this gives the candidate AI to choose companies. Quality over quantity, and a human (you) always makes the final call before anything is sent.

BRAIN

modes/*.md

Plain-language playbooks for each task: offer evaluation, apply, scan, cover letter, interview prep, negotiation. The AI follows these.

HANDS

*.mjs scripts

Zero-token deterministic tools: scan.mjs, generate-pdf.mjs, check-liveness.mjs, merge-tracker.mjs and more.

YOU

cv.md · config/profile.yml

Your data layer. Personalization lives here and is never overwritten by system updates.

MEMORY

data/ · reports/ · interview-prep/

Application tracker, per-offer evaluation reports, and an accumulating STAR+R interview story bank.

02What it does — the capabilities

Everything runs through one command, /career-ops, with sub-modes. The big ones:

🔎 Scan portals

Hits Greenhouse / Ashby / Lever / Wellfound APIs directly for 45+ pre-set companies + your custom queries. Zero LLM cost.

🧮 Evaluate an offer

Paste a JD or URL → a 6-block report (role fit, CV match, level strategy, comp research, personalization, interview prep) + a Block-G legitimacy check that flags scams & ghost jobs. Scored /5.

📄 Generate a CV PDF

ATS-optimized, keyword-injected CV rendered to PDF (also LaTeX/Overleaf export).

✉️ Cover letters

Research-backed, keyword-mirrored, with an in-chat approval gate before any PDF is made.

📊 Track everything

Every offer flows into data/applications.md with canonical statuses; a terminal dashboard lets you browse/filter.

🎤 Interview prep

Company-specific intel + a growing bank of 5–10 master STAR+Reflection stories that answer any behavioral question.

🤝 Negotiation

Salary frameworks, geo-discount pushback, competing-offer leverage scripts.

⚡ Batch mode

Evaluate many offers in parallel with headless CLI workers (claude -p / opencode run).

🌍 Language modes

Native eval modes for DE, FR, JA, AR, TR and more — used when you target non-English postings.

04How to get your job search done with it

You don't edit config by hand — you talk to the agent and it edits the files for you. Here's the loop, start to finish.

Onboard (one time, ~5 min)

Open your CLI in this folder and say: I want to set up career-ops. It'll ask for your CV (paste it, drop a LinkedIn URL, or just describe your background), your name/email/location, your target roles, and salary range. It writes cv.md, config/profile.yml, and portals.yml for you.

Teach it who you are

Tell it your "superpower," your best achievement, your deal-breakers (no on-site, no startups <20 people, etc.). The more context, the sharper the filtering. Early evaluations are rough on purpose — it gets better as it learns you.

Find roles — scan

Say /career-ops scan. It pulls fresh openings from your configured company portals at zero token cost and drops new URLs into your pipeline inbox.

Evaluate — paste & assess

Paste any job URL or JD (or /career-ops pipeline to process the inbox). You get a scored /5 report. Below 4.0/5 it will tell you not to apply — that's the point.

Apply — generate & review

For a good match: /career-ops pdf for a tailored CV, /career-ops cover for a cover letter, /career-ops apply to fill a form. It always stops before Submit — you review and send.

Track & follow up

/career-ops tracker shows your pipeline; /career-ops followup calculates cadence; /career-ops interview-prep preps you when you land an interview.

✅

Fastest path right now: open this folder in your CLI and type /career-ops to see the menu, or just say "set me up" to begin onboarding.

06Two rules baked into the system

ETHICS

Human-in-the-loop, always

It evaluates and drafts, but never submits an application for you. It also actively discourages low-fit applications (<4.0/5) — respecting both your time and the recruiter's.

DATA CONTRACT

Your data is safe from updates

A strict two-layer split (DATA_CONTRACT.md): your CV, profile, reports and tracker are never auto-touched. Only system files update. So npx @santifer/career-ops updates won't clobber your work.

💡

It's meant to be made yours. Want different archetypes, another language, custom companies, or different scoring weights? Just ask the agent — it edits the same files it reads. Say things like "add these 5 companies to my portals" or "change the archetypes to data-engineering roles."
